## Earn is Open Sourced

Superteam Earn is the best talent-matching platform on Solana. The project is fully open-sourced on [GitHub](https://github.com/SuperteamDAO/earn/issues/478) and is constantly looking for developers to contribute to open issues, some of which are fairly easy to do.

  

Why should you contribute to Earn?

1. Get [contributor](https://docs.superteam.fun/the-superteam-handbook/getting-started/community-structure) status in the Superteam of your preference! Contributors get special access to channels on Discord, and preferential entry to events. Contributor status is the perfect stepping stone to becoming a Superteam member.
2. [Get XP](https://docs.superteam.fun/the-superteam-handbook/community/the-reputation-system) if you're already a Superteam Member or Contributor
3. Unwavering love and support from the Superteam Earn team!

## Scope of Work

Earn is incentivising early contributions to certain issues with these bounties. For this bounty, you are expected to **add support for images in the listing description**.

  

See details of this GitHub issue [here](https://github.com/SuperteamDAO/earn/issues/478).

## Eligibility Requirements

1. Submit your PR link to Superteam Earn to be eligible
2. The first prize will go to the first person to successfully implement the issue and get their PR merged
3. The second prize is for the next-best attempt
4. In case there are no successful implementations of this issue, the bounty will be withdrawn
