Following the completion of the **Superteam KZ Bootcamp at AlmaU**, we are recognizing the top-performing students who demonstrated the highest level of engagement, consistency, and performance throughout the program.

This bounty is designed to reward participants who not only attended sessions, but actively contributed, built, and showed strong potential as future Web3 builders.

## **Reward Distribution**

Top performers have been selected and awarded as follows:

* **1st – 5th place** — **$300 each**
* **6th – 7th place** — **$200 each**
* **8th – 9th place** — **$50 each**
* **10th – 14th place** — **$20 each**

## **Selection Criteria**

Participants were evaluated based on:

* Attendance and consistency throughout the bootcamp
* Engagement during sessions
* Initiative and proactiveness
* Understanding of core Web3 / Solana concepts
* Overall performance and growth during the program

## **What’s Next**

This is just the beginning.

Top students will be encouraged to continue their journey by:

* participating in hackathons
* applying for grants
* joining Superteam KZ initiatives
* building real projects within the ecosystem

We believe that strong builders are shaped through execution — and this bounty is a recognition of those already on that path.
