# Official Contest Rules - Create the Future

## Official Contest Rules

#### SPONSORS

The Primary Sponsors of the 2026 Create the Future Design Contest (the “Contest”) are [COMSOL](/sponsors/12107), Inc., [Mouser Electronics](/sponsors/12040) and SAE Media Group (SMG), an SAE International affiliate company. SMG is the contest producer and owner.

#### ENTRY REQUIREMENTS

The Contest opens for entries on March 2, 2026 and closes July 1, 2026 (referred to herein as the Contest Live Period). Entries must be received by 11:59 pm EDT on July 1, 2026. For your entries to qualify for consideration, you must meet the following requirements:

* Complete the Official Entry Form and upload at least one image with your entry.
* Choose one of seven categories for your entry:
  + **Aerospace & Defense** -- Product innovations with applications in the aviation, space, and/or defense/military markets.
  + **Automotive/Transportation** -- Products that enable and improve mobility on the ground, in the air, or at sea.
  + **Electronics** -- Products that improve computing, communications, sensing, test, and other fields that rely on advances in electronic components, boards and systems; products that enable an interconnected world.
  + **Energy, Power & Propulsion** -- Products and systems that generate and deliver energy to propel vehicles on the ground, at sea, and in the air or space; that enable energy-efficient home/consumer and business/industrial power applications; or solutions for advanced energy storage.
  + **Manufacturing & Materials**  -- Products and processes that speed or streamline manufacturing and production; advances in materials and materials processing.
  + **Medical** -- Devices and systems that improve the efficiency/quality of healthcare and save or improve lives; advances in bio-medicine and drug design.
  + **Robotics & Automation** -- The design of machines that help/assist humans in a variety of fields; the application of technology, including artificial intelligence and machine learning, to automate tasks that are inefficient, difficult, or impossible for humans to perform.
* Provide a description of your entry (up to 500 words) in the form of a technical abstract, in English. Your description should cover how the entry works, what makes it novel, how it would be produced, and where it would be applied. Entries will be judged on these criteria:
  + Innovation (50% of score) -- Is the design novel? Does it represent an important advance over the current state of the art?
  + Feasibility/Manufacturability (25%) -- How easy would it be to implement? Could it be cost-effectively manufactured/produced? Has it been demonstrated to work? (Please note: prototypes are NOT required.)
  + Marketability (25%) -- Does the idea have practical applications? Is there a well-defined market for its use?
* Upload at least one (but no more than three) visual illustrations. File type options:
  + PDF(single-page), GIF, JPEG, or PNG. The visual illustration(s) should complement your entry description and can be drawings, photos, charts, or models. **File size should be kept under 2MB per illustration. Larger files can cause the entry form upload to time out.**

**Entries submitted without an illustration automatically will be disqualified, no exceptions. We cannot accept PowerPoint presentations, white papers, or other documents as illustrations. All descriptive text should be entered in the space provided on the Entry Form. Text on illustrations should be limited to captions or call outs.**

**Video:**  You also have the option of submitting video to support your entry (limited to one video per entry). Maximum length: three minutes. First, upload your video to YouTube or Vimeo. Then, when you complete the Entry Form, provide the link to the video. We cannot accept any video files by e-mail. Video is optional.

**Prototypes:** It is NOT required to have a prototype of your product. However, if a working prototype does exist, please indicate this when asked on the Entry Form. Entrants are encouraged to include an illustration of their prototype with the entry materials.

If you are having difficulty submitting your entry, please [contact us](#contactus).

#### JUDGING

There are two rounds of judging. The first will determine a) the overall Top 100 entries; b) the finalists who will move on to the second round. The second round will be a live competition. One finalist per category will be invited to New York City to present in-person to an independent judging panel. The Grand Prize and Category winners will be chosen and announced at the conclusion of these presentations. The sponsors will cover airfare and hotel costs for finalists to attend the live event. In the case of team entries, the Sponsors will cover the cost for one team member to attend. To be considered for the Grand Prize and Category prizes, finalists must attend the live competition and pitch their idea to the judges. Further details and instructions will be provided to the finalists once they are selected for the second round.

The finalists will be announced in September 2026, at which time the date and location for the live presentation round will also be announced.

SAE Media Group will recruit a minimum of 24 judges from its editorial board of readers. The judging panel also will be open to one representative from each Primary Sponsor. The judges are prohibited from entering the contest themselves or evaluating entries from any organization at which they are employed, or from family members. All decisions of the judges will be final.

#### VOTING

The Top 10 Most Popular Entries will be decided by the voting of registered users of the contest Web site. Voting will be open until September 7, 2026 (11:59 pm EDT). **These votes will have absolutely no bearing on the selection of Grand Prize or Category winners, or the Top 100 Entries, which are determined by independent panel of judges. Vote totals will only be used to determine the winners of prizes for Most Popular Entries.** Each registrant is allowed one vote per entry. Votes will not count if the registrant does not accurately complete the required fields on the registration form or if the registrant attempts to enter fraudulent data. The Contest Administrators will adjust the vote counts at any point in the competition to discount votes from registrants who have not registered properly or who attempt to vote for a single entry multiple times (including any attempt to use e-mail aliases). Voter registration data will be kept in strict confidence and used only for verification, to ensure a fair voting process.

All winners will be announced on the contest web site.
